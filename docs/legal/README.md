# CorpusMiner legal and privacy documentation

These files provide engineering requirements and draft website legal text for CorpusMiner, a product of Askit4.

They are intended to:
- make privacy, intellectual property, and legal-site requirements visible to coding agents;
- prevent legal/privacy requirements from being treated as an afterthought;
- provide starting drafts for Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookies, and legal notices;
- establish product-specific rules for uploaded academic corpora and third-party research sources.

## Important

The files under `docs/legal/templates/` or marked as draft are NOT a substitute for legal advice. Before publication, counsel should confirm at least:
- the exact legal entity operating CorpusMiner;
- governing law and jurisdiction;
- applicable Costa Rican privacy/data-protection obligations;
- whether GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or other regimes apply based on users and processing;
- data-processing roles, subprocessors, hosting locations, and cross-border transfers;
- retention periods;
- cookie/analytics behavior actually implemented;
- licensing restrictions associated with Scopus, Web of Science, uploaded PDFs, abstracts, metadata, and exports;
- commercial terms, billing, warranties, limitation of liability, and dispute-resolution language.

## Files

- `PRIVACY_AND_SECURITY_REQUIREMENTS.md` - mandatory engineering/privacy-by-design rules.
- `WEBSITE_LEGAL_REQUIREMENTS.md` - required footer links, notices, consent behavior, and release checklist.
- `INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY.md` - ownership, uploaded-content, third-party-source, and trademark rules.
- `PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md` - public-facing privacy-policy starting draft.
- `TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md` - public-facing terms starting draft.
- `COOKIE_POLICY_DRAFT.md` - public-facing cookie-policy starting draft.
- `LEGAL_NOTICE_DRAFT.md` - operator, copyright, trademark, and contact notice.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` - Scopus, Web of Science, libraries, services, and attribution guidance.
